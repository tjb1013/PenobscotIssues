Section 9.2. Adequacy of Road System & 9.3. Access into the Site

Vehicular access to the site must be on roads which have adequate capacity... intersections on major access routes... must function at a minimum at Level of Service D after development. Any driveway or proposed street must be designed so as to provide the minimum sight distance... Private entrances/exits must be located at least fifty (50) feet from the closest unsignalized intersection...

Section 9.6. Parking Layout and Design

Parking areas with more than two (2) parking spaces must be arranged so that it is not necessary for vehicles to back into the street. All parking spaces, access drives, and impervious surfaces must be located at least five (5) feet from any side or rear lot line...

Section 9.22. Special Features of the Development

All features of the development which may have an adverse audio/visual impact on surrounding properties shall have sufficient setbacks and screening to minimize that impact. Hours of operations, noise levels and exterior lighting will be designed to minimize adverse impact on neighboring properties and public ways. Lighting shall be arranged to minimize glare and reflection on adjacent properties and the traveling public.
