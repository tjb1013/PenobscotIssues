Surry Ordinance Excerpts

Note: This is an excerpted reference file containing specific commercial performance standards.

UDO Section IV.1: Buffer Strips

No commercial use may be erected or any use permitted unless a buffer strip at least thirty (30) feet wide is provided and maintained between any adjoining residential district or use and the non-residential structure or use. In addition, buffer strips can be used to minimize the noise of different land uses. When natural features... do not exist or are insufficient to provide a buffer, the developer shall provide a landscaped area at least 30 feet wide. Where such landscaping is not feasible... the developer may provide a fence at least 6 feet high...

UDO Section IV.14: Exterior Lighting

All exterior lighting shall be designed to encourage energy efficiency, to ensure safe movement of people and vehicles, and to minimize adverse impact on neighboring properties and public ways. All lighting must encourage dark skies. Adverse impact is to be judged in terms of hazards to people and vehicular traffic, and potential damage to the value of adjacent properties.

UDO Section IV.18: Off-Street Parking

It shall be unlawful to use any required off-street parking or loading facilities for motor vehicle repair work... No area designated as a required parking area in connection with any designated building or use shall be operated as a commercial or public parking lot... Restaurants, eating & drinking establishments: 1 per 4 seats plus 1 per shift employee.
