Brooklin Ordinance Excerpts

Note: This is an excerpted reference file containing specific commercial performance standards.

Section 9.1. Utilization of the Site

Buildings must be set back at least twenty five (25) feet from the edge of traveled portions of existing town ways and fifty (50) feet from the edge of traveled portions of a state highway, and must have minimum side and rear lot setbacks of fifteen (15) feet. Buildings shall not exceed thirty-five (35) feet in height.

Section 9.2. Adequacy of Road System & 9.3 Access

Vehicular access to the site must be on roads which have adequate capacity... The applicant must have a letter from the Brooklin Fire Chief stating that the development meets the needs of the Fire Department. Points of access and egress must be located to avoid hazardous conflicts... Private entrances/exits must be located at least fifty (50) feet from the closest intersection.

Section 9.9. Water Supply & 9.10 Sewage Disposal

Uses that require extraction of more than 1000 gallons daily of ground water must provide a written report of a hydrologic investigation conducted and prepared by a licensed professional... The development is provided with a method of disposing of sewage which is in compliance with the State Plumbing Code.

Section 9.22. Exterior Lighting

Lighting fixtures shall be shielded or hooded so that the lighting elements are not exposed to normal view of motorists, pedestrians or buildings on abutting properties. Direct or indirect illumination shall not exceed 0.5 foot-candles at the lot line.

Section 9.23. Noise

Land uses involving noise in excess of that which is normal for the area are designed and landscaped to minimize noise interference with neighboring uses. Noise emanating from the proposed use shall not exceed the standards set forth in Appendix B "Noise Control" of the Town of Brooklin Wind Energy Facility Ordinance.

Section 9.26. Signage

Signs shall be no larger than sixteen (16) square feet in area, shall not exceed two (2) signs per premises, and shall not extend more than fifteen (15) feet above the ground. Signs shall be illuminated only by shielded, non-flashing lights...
