Land Use Regulation - Section 5: Uses

Commercial Structures are Prohibited ("No") in the Resource Protection and Limited Residential-Recreational Districts. Commercial Structures require a Planning Board (PB) Permit in the General Development District. (Note: There are no specific decibel limits, lighting limits, or operational hour limits attached to this commercial approval process in the text of the ordinance.)

Land Use Regulation - Section 7: Structures (7.4 & 7.5)

7.4 Residential structures located in the General Development District or Limited Residential-Recreational District shall be set back a minimum of 50 feet from any highway or roads in subdivisions.

7.5 There shall be a 20 foot setback on the side lot lines and rear lot lines of all structures in any District.
