Sedgwick Ordinance Excerpts

Note: This is an excerpted reference file containing specific commercial performance standards.

Site Plan Review Ordinance - Section II.B: Relationship of Proposed Building

Storage areas, building machinery, loading areas, utility buildings and similar structures shall have sufficient setbacks and screening to minimize their adverse impact on other land use within the development area and on surrounding properties.

Site Plan Review Ordinance - Section III.B.1: Off-Street Parking

Restaurants, eating and drinking establishments: 1 parking space for every 4 seats, plus 1 space for 2 employees.

Special Amusement Permit Ordinance - Section 2.I.3: Time Limits

Time Limits: All outdoor permitted activities at the permitted premises shall cease at 9 pm. All indoor permitted activities at the permitted premises shall cease at 11 pm.
