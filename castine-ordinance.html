Castine Ordinance Excerpts

Note: This is an excerpted reference file containing specific commercial performance standards.

Section 6.9. Fire Hazard Mitigation in the Commercial District

There shall be no change of use of a structure and no structure shall be built, moved, replaced, altered or enlarged without consultation with and approval in writing by the Fire Chief... Fire trucks and ambulances shall have unobstructed access to the structure... Adequate water supply for fire suppression is or will be available prior to granting a certificate of occupancy.

Section 6.14. Lighting

No land use or establishment shall be permitted to produce unreasonable glare or brightness (0.3 foot-candles) beyond its lot lines. Where practicable, lighting shall be directed downward and the light source shall be shielded. All exterior lighting shall be designed to ensure safe movement of people and vehicles, and to minimize adverse impact on neighboring properties...

Section 6.21. Noise

No land use or establishment shall be permitted to produce unreasonable noise perceptible beyond their lot lines... Unreasonable noise shall be noise exceeding the following levels measured on an ANSI Type 1 or 2 meter using the A-weighted network: 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. - 60 dBA; 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. - 50 dBA.

Section 6.22. Off-Street Parking (B.12)

Restaurants and cocktail lounges: one (1) space for each four (4) customer seats, plus one (1) space for each two (2) employees.
